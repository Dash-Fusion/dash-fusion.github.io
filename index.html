Why they are built this way

Small apps that mind their own business.

Works offline

Every app runs in airplane mode. There is no server to go down and nothing to wait for.

No accounts

No sign-up, no email, no password. Install the app and start using it.

Your data stays put

Notes, budgets, counters and game progress are stored on your device. Your content is never uploaded.

The apps are free and paid for by ads. Google AdMob serves those ads and may use your advertising ID; Potion Sort additionally uses Google Firebase for anonymous crash reports and gameplay statistics. Each app's privacy policy sets out exactly what that covers.
